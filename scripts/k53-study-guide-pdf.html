Free K53 Learners Study Guide

A quick, original summary of the K53 learner's licence test structure, pass marks, licence categories and booking checklist.

1. Know the three test sections

Rules of the Road
Speed limits, right of way, stopping, overtaking and safe road use.
Road Traffic Signs
Regulatory, warning and guidance signs, signals and road markings.
Vehicle Controls
Pedals, steering, brakes, switches, instruments and warning lights.

2. Learn the pass marks

SectionQuestionsMinimum correct
Rules of the Road2822 out of 28
Road Traffic Signs2823 out of 28
Vehicle Controls86 out of 8
You must pass all three sections. Failing one section means failing the test.

3. Choose the correct learner's licence category

  • Code 1 — motorcycles: minimum age 16 for 125 cc or less; minimum age 18 for over 125 cc.
  • Code 2 — light motor vehicles: minimum age 17; GVM not exceeding 3,500 kg.
  • Code 3 — heavy motor vehicles: minimum age 18; GVM exceeding 3,500 kg.

Code 8, Code 10 and Code 14 are familiar driving-licence terms often used when searching for study material. Confirm the official Code 1, 2 or 3 learner category with your DLTC when booking.

A learner's licence is valid for 24 months and cannot be extended.

4. Booking checklist

  1. Choose Code 1, Code 2 or Code 3 and check the minimum age.
  2. Book with your nearest Driving Licence Testing Centre (DLTC). Gauteng residents should use the official eNaTIS portal.
  3. Take your ID, required ID photos, proof of postal and residential address, booking fee and completed LL1 form.
  4. Complete an eye test at the DLTC or submit the required form from a qualified optometrist.
  5. Bring your booking receipt and ID on the test day.
  6. After passing, pay the issue fee. Fees and photo requirements vary, so confirm them with your DLTC.

5. A simple study plan

  1. Read one Rules of the Road or road-sign topic.
  2. Write down unfamiliar terms and signs.
  3. Take an exam-style practice test.
  4. Review every wrong answer before trying again.
  5. Practise all three sections; do not rely on one combined score.
